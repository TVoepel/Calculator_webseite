All Supported Area Units

With this area converter, you can directly convert square millimeters, square centimeters, square meters, ares, hectares, square kilometers, square inches, square feet, square yards, acres, and square miles.

Hectares and ares are the standard units for land area in Germany and the EU, while acres are common in English-speaking countries.

In Practice: When You Need to Convert Areas

When buying land or property in the US or the UK, areas are often given in acres instead of hectares or square meters – one acre equals roughly 4,047 m².

In agriculture, hectares and ares are the standard measures for fields and forest land, while private gardens are usually measured in square meters.

US real estate listings (living space given in square feet rather than square meters) are also easier to judge realistically once converted.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many square meters are in a hectare?

1 hectare (ha) equals exactly 10,000 m² – the area of a square with 100 m sides.

How many square meters are in an acre?

1 acre equals 4046.8564224 m², which is a bit less than half a hectare (about 40.5 ares).

What's the difference between an are and a hectare?

1 are equals 100 m², and 1 hectare equals 100 ares (10,000 m²). The are is used less often, mainly for smaller plots of land or gardens.

How many square meters is a soccer field?

A soccer field at FIFA standard dimensions (about 105 × 68 m) covers roughly 7,140 m², or just under 0.7 hectares – a common reference value for area comparisons.

How do I convert square feet to square meters?

1 square foot (ft²) equals 0.09290304 m². A 2,000-square-foot apartment in the US is therefore about 186 m².
