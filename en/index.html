Why this unit converter?

All conversion factors are based on official, precisely defined SI and imperial standards (e.g. 1 inch = 0.0254 m, 1 pound = 0.45359237 kg, 1 US gallon = 3.785411784 L). Where values are inherently only approximations (e.g. fuel energy content, speed of sound), this is clearly noted on the respective page.

The converters run entirely in your browser, without any server request and without storing your input.
