Frequently Asked Questions

Which US size does women's EU size 40 correspond to?

EU/DE 40 corresponds to approximately US size 8 – but the exact fit depends heavily on the manufacturer and cut.

Which UK/US size does men's EU size 50 correspond to?

EU 50 corresponds to approximately UK/US size 40 for men's outerwear (jackets, suits), which is roughly letter size M to L.

Why do clothing sizes differ between different online shops?

There is no binding, globally uniform standard for clothing sizes. Each manufacturer sets its own cut, fit, and size labeling, so the same number can turn out differently depending on the brand.

Which EU size does Italian size 44 correspond to?

Italian women's sizes tend to run with higher numbers: IT 44 corresponds to approximately EU/DE 38. The most reliable approach is comparing actual body measurements (bust, waist, hip) with the size chart of the specific manufacturer.

How do I find the right size when shopping online?

Measure your bust, waist, and hip circumference and compare them with the brand-specific size chart in the shop – this is usually more accurate than a general conversion table, since cuts vary between brands.
