Formula & Reference Values

Result = Value × Factor(from) ÷ Factor(to), base unit = 1 meter 1 inch = 0.0254 m (exact, international definition) 1 foot = 0.3048 m (exact) 1 yard = 0.9144 m (exact) 1 mile = 1,609.344 m (exact) 1 nautical mile (nm) = 1,852 m (exact, internationally defined) 1 ångström (Å) = 0.0000000001 m

Example: 1 kilometer = 1,000 meters. So if you convert 2.5 kilometers to meters, you get 2,500 meters.

Sources: BIPM – SI Brochure (International System of Units), NIST – Guide to Unit Conversion, Wikipedia – Meter

All supported length units

With this length converter you can directly convert nanometers, micrometers, millimeters, centimeters, meters, kilometers, inches (in), feet (ft), yards, miles, nautical miles, and ångströms.

The metric factors are based on the SI system; the imperial conversions (inch, foot, yard, mile) correspond to the values exactly defined internationally since 1959 (International Yard and Pound Agreement).

In practice: when you need to convert lengths

Inches to centimeters is the conversion you'll need most often when buying TVs, monitors, and laptops – screen diagonals are almost always given in inches, while furniture and alcoves are measured in cm.

When traveling to the US or the UK, converting miles to kilometers helps you judge distances and speed limits (e.g. 65 mph) correctly. Shipping and aviation also navigate using nautical miles.

Inch and foot measurements also turn up with American furniture, pipe diameters, tire sizes, or shoe sizes – none of which compare directly to the metric system.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many centimeters is an inch?

1 inch equals exactly 2.54 cm. This definition has been fixed internationally since 1959 (International Yard and Pound Agreement).

How do I convert inches to cm, for example for a TV?

Multiply the number of inches by 2.54. A 55-inch TV therefore has a screen diagonal of 55 × 2.54 = 139.7 cm.

How many kilometers is a mile?

1 mile equals exactly 1.609344 km. The US speed limit of 65 mph therefore corresponds to roughly 104.6 km/h.

What is the difference between a mile and a nautical mile?

The statute mile is defined as 1,609.344 m, the nautical mile as 1,852 m. Nautical miles are used in shipping and aviation and relate to the degree division of the Earth's circumference.

Why does the US use inches and feet instead of meters?

The US never fully switched to the metric system and traditionally uses the Anglo-American system of measurement (imperial/US customary units) for everyday life, construction, and many product specifications.
