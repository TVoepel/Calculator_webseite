All supported weight units

With this weight converter you can directly convert milligrams, grams, kilograms, Zentner, Doppelzentner, decitonne, tonnes, pounds (lb), ounces (oz), stone, carats, US tons, and UK tons.

The imperial conversion factors (pound, ounce, stone) correspond to the internationally standardized avoirdupois system.

In practice: when you need to convert weights

In strength training, weight plates and US gym equipment are often labeled in pounds (lb), while body weight and training plans in Germany are usually given in kilograms.

Free baggage allowances on flights are often limited in pounds by US airlines, and gold jewelry and diamonds are weighed in carats – both convert quickly to grams or kilograms with this tool.

US recipes also sometimes give ingredients in ounces (oz) instead of grams, which can cause confusion when cooking European or American recipes.

In agriculture, crop yields are traditionally given in Zentner or Doppelzentner per hectare (e.g. "80 dt/ha of wheat"), while official statistics use the decitonne (dt) as the official unit – both figures correspond to exactly 100 kg.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many kilograms is 1 pound?

1 pound (lb, avoirdupois) equals exactly 0.45359237 kg. Conversely, 1 kg is about 2.2046 pounds.

How many grams is an ounce?

1 ounce (oz) equals exactly 0.028349523125 kg, or about 28.35 grams – one sixteenth of a pound.

What is a carat in gold weight?

1 carat (metric carat, ct) equals 0.2 grams. Not to be confused with "karat" as a measure of gold purity (e.g. 24 karat = pure gold), which has a different meaning.

How much does 1 stone weigh in kilograms?

1 stone equals 6.35029318 kg (14 pounds). The unit is used mainly in the UK and Ireland for body weight.

What is the difference between a US ton, UK ton, and metric tonne?

The metric tonne (t) is 1,000 kg, the US ton (short ton) is 907.18474 kg, and the UK ton (long ton) is 1,016.0469088 kg – a common stumbling block in international freight figures.

What is the difference between Zentner, Doppelzentner, and decitonne?

1 Zentner equals 50 kg in Germany. 1 Doppelzentner (Dz) is 100 kg, i.e. two Zentner. The decitonne (dt) is also exactly 100 kg (one tenth of a tonne) and is used mainly in official agricultural statistics, for example for crop yields in dt/ha – Doppelzentner and decitonne therefore denote the same amount.
