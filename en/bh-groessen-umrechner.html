Formula & reference values

Band size (underbust measurement), base = US/UK band size in inches EU/DE band size = US band size × 2.5 − 10 France band size = US band size × 2.5 + 10 Italy band size = (US band size − 28) ÷ 2 + 1 Cup size: each letter/step ≈ 2.5 cm (1 inch) of difference between bust and underbust measurement. Identical up to cup D in all systems. From cup DD/E onward: US DD/E = UK DD = DE/EU/FR/IT E US DDD/F = UK E = DE/EU/FR/IT F US G = UK F = DE/EU/FR/IT G US H = UK FF = DE/EU/FR/IT H

Example: US/UK 34D corresponds to DE/EU 75D, France 95D and Italy 4D. US/UK 34DD/E, on the other hand, corresponds to DE/EU 75E, but UK 34DD (not "E").

All supported bra size systems

Supported band size systems: Germany/EU (60–100), France (80–120), Italy (1–9), USA/UK (28–44). Supported cup systems: continental Europe (DE/EU/FR/IT: AA–H), USA (AA–H, including DD/E and DDD/F), UK (AA–FF).

Germany follows the European band size standard directly, so DE and EU values are always identical. France and Italy use their own, different numbering for the same physical size.

In practice: when you need to convert bra sizes

When shopping online at international lingerie brands (US, UK, France, Italy), sizes are often given only in the respective country's system – a conversion mistake on the cup size quickly leads to a badly fitting bra.

While traveling or shopping abroad, the conversion helps you find the right size in-store without having to try it on.

When comparing size charts from different manufacturers, it also helps to know that an "E" cup in continental Europe is not the same as an "E" cup in the USA.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert a US bra size to an EU size?

The band size converts using EU band size = US band size × 2.5 − 10, so US 34 becomes EU band size 75. The cup size stays the same up to cup D (A–D are identical); from cup DD/E onward the letters diverge between countries.

Is the German bra size the same as the EU size?

Yes. Germany uses the European band size numbering directly (underbust measurement in cm, rounded to the nearest 5 cm) with no deviation. France and Italy, on the other hand, use their own offset numbering for the same physical size.

Why do cup letters differ between countries?

Up to cup D, the USA, UK and continental Europe (DE/EU/FR/IT) use the same letters. From cup DD/E onward, historically different letter sequences developed: US DD/E corresponds to UK DD and EU/FR/IT E, US DDD/F corresponds to UK E and EU/FR/IT F, and so on.

How much bigger is one cup size than the next?

Each cup step corresponds to about 2.5 cm (1 inch) of difference between bust and underbust measurement. AA stands for 0 cm difference, A for about 2.5 cm, B for about 5 cm, C for about 7.5 cm, and so on.

How do I measure my bra size correctly?

For the band size, measure the underbust horizontally right below the bust; for the cup, measure the bust at its fullest point. The difference between the two measurements determines the cup size.
