Frequently Asked Questions

Which US size does EU size 38 correspond to?

EU 38 corresponds to approximately US women's 7.5 or US men's 6 – but the exact size depends on the manufacturer, since there is no uniform worldwide standard for shoe sizes.

Why are US shoe sizes numbered differently than EU sizes?

Historically, Europe uses the Paris point system (1 point = 2/3 cm of last length), while the US uses the Barleycorn system (1 unit = 1/3 inch ≈ 8.47 mm) with its own zero point – which results in different number sequences for the same foot length.

Why does one brand run larger than another at the same stated size?

There is no binding international standard that all manufacturers must follow. Each brand uses its own last (the basic shape of the shoe), so the same stated size can turn out differently depending on the manufacturer.

How do I measure my foot length correctly?

Stand on a sheet of paper, mark the position of your heel and longest toe, and measure the distance in centimeters – ideally in the evening, since feet swell slightly over the course of the day. Measure both feet, since they often differ in size, and go by the larger one.

How does children's shoe size change with age?

Children's feet grow irregularly; roughly speaking, new shoes are needed every 2-4 months. It's more useful to regularly measure the current foot length than to rely on fixed age-based sizes.
