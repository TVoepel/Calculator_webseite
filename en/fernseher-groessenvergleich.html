What is a TV size comparison useful for?

When buying a new TV, this comparison helps you picture its actual size in your living room – inch measurements alone are hard for most people to visualize.

Exact width and height also matter for wall mounting, to plan enough clearance from furniture, outlets, or shelves. If you're upgrading from an older set to a larger new model, you can see the size difference directly here.

For home theater planning and projector screens, you can also switch the aspect ratio to compare cinema formats (21:9) or older 4:3 devices.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate a TV's width and height from its inch size?

The inch measurement is the screen diagonal. Using the Pythagorean theorem, you can calculate width and height from a known aspect ratio (usually 16:9): Width = Diagonal × 16 ÷ √(16²+9²), Height = Diagonal × 9 ÷ √(16²+9²). The result in inches is then converted to centimeters by multiplying by 2.54.

How big is a 55-inch TV in cm?

A 55-inch TV (16:9) has a diagonal of 139.7 cm and measures about 121.8 × 68.5 cm (width × height) for the active screen area, excluding the frame/bezel.

How much bigger is a 100-inch TV compared to a 55-inch model?

A 100-inch screen has about 3.3 times the area of a 55-inch screen at the same aspect ratio, even though the diagonal is only around 1.8 times as long – area grows with the square of the diagonal, not linearly.

What viewing distance is recommended for which screen size?

As a rough guideline for 4K TVs, independent test sites such as RTINGS.com suggest a viewing distance of about 1.0 to 1.5 times the screen diagonal, and about 2 to 3 times for Full HD sets. These are general guidelines – personal preference and eyesight also play a role.

Why do the calculated dimensions differ slightly from manufacturer specs?

This calculator assumes an exact 16:9 aspect ratio for the active screen area. Manufacturers sometimes round inch measurements, and depending on the bezel, the overall dimensions of the device can differ from the pure screen diagonal.
